Our story

Mulewa Academy started in 2012, when our founder was at a camp and saw that kids far from town often had nothing to do after school. They would join people smoking. He started the academy to keep them busy with productive sports — and it worked. It has produced successful players.

Today we are FAZ registered (since 2014) and proud to count Trade Kings, Jumble, Ross Breed, Hybrid and Billy Sports as partners. Our mission is to further develop soccer in Zambia, on and off the pitch.
